Court Rules

Help us keep Dink & Drive Court safe, clean, and enjoyable for everyone.

1. Personal Belongings

Keep your personal belongings secure. Dink & Drive Court is not responsible for lost or damaged items.

2. Food & Drinks

Food and drinks are not allowed inside the playing area. Water is allowed, but clean spills immediately.

3. Clean As You Go

Dispose of trash properly and leave the court and surrounding areas clean after your session.

4. Proper Court Shoes

Only proper non-marking court shoes or suitable sports shoes are allowed.

5. Events & Gatherings

Events, tournaments, parties, and large gatherings require prior management approval.

6. Noise

Keep noise at a reasonable level and respect nearby residents.

7. Respect & Sportsmanship

Treat players, staff, and guests with respect. Aggressive or abusive behavior is not tolerated.

8. Court Time

Use the court only during your reserved time and leave promptly when your booking ends.

9. Equipment & Facilities

Take care of the net, lights, benches, and other facilities. Misuse damage may be charged.

10. No Cancellation / No Refund

Confirmed bookings are non-cancellable and non-refundable. Unsafe weather may allow rescheduling, subject to availability and management approval.

11. Booking Confirmation

A booking request is not confirmed until availability has been checked and confirmation has been provided.

12. Guests & Spectators

Guests and spectators are welcome if they follow the rules and do not interfere with players.

13. Safety

Follow safety instructions and report unsafe conditions to management immediately.

14. Management Rights

Management may stop or refuse a session when necessary for safety, conduct, or court operations.

Thank you for helping us maintain a clean, safe, and enjoyable environment. 🏓
