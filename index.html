Court Rules

Please help us keep the court safe, clean, and enjoyable for everyone.

1

Personal Belongings: Dink & Drive Court is not responsible for lost or damaged personal belongings.

2

Food & Drinks: No food or drinks inside the playing area. Water is allowed, but any spills must be cleaned immediately.

3

Clean As You Go: Dispose of your trash properly and leave the court clean after your session.

4

Proper Shoes: Only proper non-marking court or sports shoes are allowed. Do not use shoes that can damage or mark the court surface.

5

Events & Gatherings: Events, tournaments, parties, and large gatherings require prior management approval.

6

Noise: Keep noise at a reasonable level and respect nearby residents.

7

Respect & Sportsmanship: Aggressive, abusive, threatening, or disrespectful behavior is not allowed.

8

Reserved Time: Use the court only during your reserved time and leave promptly when your session ends.

9

Take Care of the Facilities: Please take care of the net, lights, benches, and other court facilities. Damage caused by misuse may be charged.

10

No Cancellation / No Refund: Bookings are non-refundable. If unsafe weather conditions prevent play, the booking may be rescheduled to another available date and time, subject to availability and management approval.

11

Booking Confirmation: A booking request is confirmed only after Dink & Drive Court checks availability and confirms the booking.

12

Guests & Spectators: Guests and spectators are allowed provided they follow the court rules and do not interfere with players.

13

Safety & Conduct: Management reserves the right to stop or refuse a session if safety, conduct, or normal court operations are affected.

14

Enjoy the Game: Play fairly, respect other players, and help keep Dink & Drive Court a welcoming place for everyone.
