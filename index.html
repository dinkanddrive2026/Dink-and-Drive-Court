Whether you're practicing your shots, playing with friends, or joining a game, our court is ready for you.

Court Rules

Please follow these rules to keep Dink & Drive Court safe, clean, and enjoyable for everyone.

1. Personal Belongings

Please keep your personal belongings secure. Dink & Drive Court is not responsible for lost or damaged items.

2. Food & Drinks

Food and drinks are not allowed inside the playing area. Water is allowed, but please clean up any spills immediately.

3. Clean As You Go

Keep the court and surrounding areas clean. Dispose of trash properly and leave the area clean after your session.

4. Proper Court Shoes

Only proper non-marking court shoes or suitable sports shoes are allowed. Shoes that may damage or mark the court are not permitted.

5. Events & Gatherings

Events, tournaments, parties, or large gatherings require prior approval from management.

6. Noise

Please keep noise at a reasonable level and respect other players and nearby residents.

7. Respect & Sportsmanship

Treat players, staff, and guests with respect. Aggressive, abusive, or disrespectful behavior is not tolerated.

8. Court Time

Use the court only during your reserved time and leave promptly when your booking ends.

9. Equipment & Facilities

Please take care of the court, net, lights, benches, and other facilities. Damage caused by misuse may be charged.

10. No Cancellation / No Refund

Confirmed bookings are non-cancellable and non-refundable. If unsafe weather prevents play, the booking may be rescheduled to another available date and time, subject to availability and management approval.

11. Booking Confirmation

A booking request is not considered confirmed until availability has been checked and confirmation has been provided.

12. Guests & Spectators

Guests and spectators are welcome but must follow the court rules and must not interfere with players.

13. Safety

Follow all safety instructions and report any unsafe conditions to management immediately.

14. Management Rights

Management may stop or refuse a session if necessary for safety, proper conduct, or normal court operations.

Thank you for helping us maintain a clean, safe, and enjoyable environment for everyone. 🏓
